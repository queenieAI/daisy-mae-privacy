Daisy Mae Bot Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 24, 2026

Daisy Mae Bot is a Discord application designed to provide services and automation for the Animal Crossing: New Horizons community.

1. Information We May Access

When you interact with Daisy Mae Bot, the Bot may access information provided through Discord that is necessary to provide its functionality, including:

  • Discord user ID
  • Discord username and display name
  • Discord server information
  • Information about interactions with the Bot
  • Commands and message content intentionally sent to the Bot
  • Information necessary to send requested responses or direct messages

2. How We Use Information

Daisy Mae Bot may use this information to:

  • Process and respond to user commands
  • Provide Animal Crossing: New Horizons community features
  • Process requests for Treasure Islands
  • Provide Dodo Codes to users who request them
  • Send requested information through Discord direct messages
  • Operate and maintain the Bot
  • Prevent abuse, spam, or unauthorized use
  • Troubleshoot technical problems

We do not sell users' personal information or use Discord information for advertising or unrelated profiling.

3. Message Content

Daisy Mae Bot may process message content when necessary to identify and respond to supported commands.

For example, when a user intentionally sends a command requesting a Dodo Code or another Bot feature, Daisy Mae Bot processes the command so it can provide the requested service.

Message content is used for the Bot's functionality and is not intentionally used to monitor unrelated conversations.

4. Direct Messages and Dodo Codes

Daisy Mae Bot may send users information through Discord direct messages when the user requests a feature that uses private delivery.

Dodo Codes may be sent through direct messages rather than publicly posted in a Discord channel to reduce unnecessary exposure of the codes.

5. Information Storage and Retention

Daisy Mae Bot only retains information when necessary for the operation and maintenance of the Bot.

Limited information such as Discord user IDs, server IDs, command-related information, or Bot configuration information may be stored depending on the Bot's features and configuration.

Information that is no longer necessary may be deleted.

6. Information Sharing

Daisy Mae Bot does not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the Bot, such as hosting or infrastructure providers.

7. Discord

Daisy Mae Bot operates through Discord. Discord independently processes information according to its own policies.

View Discord's Privacy Policy

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by Daisy Mae Bot from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

9. User Privacy Requests

Users may contact the Bot's developer with questions about information associated with their interaction with Daisy Mae Bot or to request deletion of information that we control.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Bot's functionality or data practices change. The Last Updated date will be changed when material updates are made.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests regarding Daisy Mae Bot, please contact the Bot's developer through the Discord community where Daisy Mae Bot is operated.
